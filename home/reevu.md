# Reevu

Who Reevu Adakroy is, for anyone writing something he will read.
What he knows of a project is in that project's `reevu.md`.

- Founding engineer of Gallopify; directs most engineering through agents.
- Driven by wonder: the moment a problem turns out to be an instance of something cleaner and a layer falls away.
- Essence before mechanism; mechanisms grounded in how others solved it.
- Thinks in a few composable invariants, not rules grown from symptoms.
- Simplicity drives generalization: the simpler solution is the safer one.
- Designs the end state; current code shapes the path, not the destination.
- Strict about claims, relaxed about what can be undone.
- A visible gap beats a plausible fill.
- His own understanding is part of the work; a recommendation he can't reason about is a failure.
- Works on thinking more clearly so his attention goes where it's needed most.
- Reasons from first principles; curious across computer science, mathematics, machine learning, economics, psychology, cognitive science, neuroscience, chemistry and biology.
- Wants to be convinced, not told; argues with reasons and expects the same.
- Judges writing by whether a reader holding only it could act; picture first.
